The pick: 1a, the Shrine Sheet
It is the only one of the three sized to the reward. Eleven times a run, a tenth of a level-up's value: a sheet that rises, reads in two seconds and drops. The pole and the hero stay in view, so the giver is the real pole glowing in the world, not a painting of it. Nothing on the sheet is a title. It needs the least new art (eleven carved-face icons, which 1b and 1c need too) and it is the one that still works on a 20:9 phone, where it simply shows more world.
THE SHEET
Full width, y 1220–1920 (700 tall, bottom-anchored; taller phones add world above, never sheet). Fill #E2CFA4, top border 6 px #241B14. Crest: the pole's thunderbird painting, 320 wide, centred, top at y 1130 so it straddles the edge. Label "TOTEM 4 OF 11": Bitter 600 26, tracking +15 %, #5A4A3A, at (48, 1258). First-totem hint "Pick one. It stays all run.": Bitter italic 26, #5A4A3A, right-aligned at x 1032, same line; never shown again that run.
THE WORLD
Dim rgba(48,30,14,.5) over everything above the sheet, HUD included. The HUD root offsets by the safe-area inset, so on cutout phones the rail and plates start at y 130; the sheet is bottom-anchored and unaffected. The pole gets a turquoise glow (#3F8F8A, radius 270, alpha .35 → .6, 1.6 s loop) while the sheet is up; the ring on the sand holds full. On close the glow dies in 400 ms and the pole desaturates to grey over the same 400 ms.
THE ROW · the whole row is the button
984 × 170 at x 48; y 1330, 1520, 1710 (gap 20). Fill #F3E6C8, border 4 px #241B14, hard shadow 6 px #241B14, no radius, padding 0 24. Plate 120 × 120: #3F8F8A, border 4 px ink, radius 10, carved-face icon 96 inside. Text column from x 192: number Alfa Slab One 52 #241B14 (#A8792A on a top-third roll) and stat Bitter 600 32 #241B14 on one baseline, 14 px apart; under it the spirit, Bitter italic 26 #5A4A3A: "Le-loo, the Wolf" (word, comma, animal; word alone when there is none). Gauge 220 wide at the right edge: track 14 tall, #E2CFA4, 3 px ink border, split in three by 3 px ink lines, filled thirds #C9932F; range ends under it in Bitter 600 18 #5A4A3A.
THE ROLL
Thirds of the gift's own range: 1, 2 or 3 segments. The ends are always written (+1% … +3%), so a full gauge on speed never outranks an empty one on damage. On a top-third roll the number turns brass dark. No feathers, no stars, no seal: the gifts have no rarity and the screen must not invent one.
OPEN · 420 ms
Pie completes → ring flashes turquoise 120 ms → dim 0 → .5 over 150 ms while the sheet slides up from y 1920 to 1220 in 260 ms (cubic ease-out) → rows slide in from +40 px, 180 ms each, staggered 50 ms → crest drops 24 px into place, 200 ms, 8 % overshoot. The glow loop starts with the sheet.
PRESS · 60 ms
Row translates (6, 6), shadow to 0, 6 px #C9932F inner outline, plate glow 0 0 34 #3F8F8A; the other two rows fade to 60 %. Release inside the row takes it; drag out cancels (90 ms back). A tap anywhere else does nothing: there is no way out without a pick.
CLOSE · 380 ms
The plate lifts off the row and flies to the toast slot (540, 300) in 300 ms ease-in-out, shrinking 120 → 72; the sheet slides down 220 ms (cubic ease-in); dim lifts 150 ms. The toast "Le-loo · +2% move speed" with the face holds 1.2 s. Control returns at 300 ms; the hero is safe for 1 s as today.
CUT, AND WHY
Eyebrow and title: the pole and the label say "totem" already. Subline: a rule, taught once. The painting: replaced by the real pole glowing. The disc: the number moved into the line. The feathers: replaced by the gauge. The two decorative feathers: gone. Added: the count, so eleven small gifts read as one accumulating thing.
Critique, in short
VERDICT PER ELEMENT
The dimKeep, lighter (50 %) and only above the sheet. The eyebrowCut. Merged with the count into one label. The titleCut. The glowing pole and the label name the screen. The sublineCut, except on the first totem of a run. The paintingRework: the real pole glows; the thunderbird becomes the sheet's crest. The planksRework into paper rows in the kit. The discMerge into the line: the number leads it. The wordKeep, smaller, second: "Le-loo, the Wolf". The animalMerge onto the same line as the word. The lineRework as the headline: number slab, stat bold. The feathersCut. A range gauge with both ends written.
FROM THE GENRE
Megabonk: the shrine stays in the world and the pick is a panel, not a screen. Hades: the giver is present and the effect is one line. Vampire Survivors: icon, name, one line, read in a glance. Archero: a smaller moment than a level-up, scaled to the favour. Brotato: the number is the headline. Not taken from any of them: rarity frames, because these rolls are not rarity.

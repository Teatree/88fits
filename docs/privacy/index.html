Outlaw Survivors

Privacy Policy · effective 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Outlaw Survivors ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: there are no accounts, we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and gameplay statistics are only sent if you agree to it.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked heroes and items, gold, settings, upgrades) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Gameplay statistics (only with your consent)

The first time you start the game it asks whether you want to share play data. If you choose No thanks, nothing is sent. If you agree, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to help us balance and improve it:

WhatExamples
Gameplay eventsruns started and finished, the stage, hero, weapons and upgrades chosen, time survived, levels, gold earned and spent
Run records and replaysa compact record of a run (the choices made and the movement inputs), so a run can be replayed for balancing and bug fixing
Performanceframe rate and frame times
Device informationoperating system and version, device model, screen size, language
A random player IDan ID the game creates on first start (not linked to your Google account, phone number or advertising ID), and a generated nickname made from it, such as "Dusty Coyote 41"
Survey answersif you choose to answer an optional survey after a run, your answers, including anything you type

We do not collect your location: location lookup from your IP address is switched off for every event.

The statistics are processed by our analytics provider, PostHog, on servers in the European Union (posthog.com/privacy). They are used only to understand how the game is played and to fix and improve it. We do not sell them and do not use them for advertising.

Changing your mind: you can turn play data off at any time in the game's Settings. Surveys can be turned off there too, or with "Don't ask me again".

3. Advertising

The game is planned to offer optional rewarded ads (for example "watch an ad to get back up"). Third-party ads are not shown yet. Before they are added, this policy will be updated to name the ad provider and explain what it collects, and players in regions that require it will be asked for consent.

4. Google Play

The game is downloaded and updated through Google Play, which handles its own data under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has shared play data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Gameplay statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the game. Because there are no accounts, the data is linked only to the random player ID. To have your data deleted, contact us with the player name shown in the game's Settings (next to the version number) and we will delete everything linked to it.

7. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play page.

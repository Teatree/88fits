Before the game

01Loading

game/ui/Boot.gd

Show that the game is loading, without an icon or a bar: the moving wallpaper is the progress indicator.

When it appears

The first scene at app start. Godot's boot splash and the first frame are one plain dark colour. The dark wallpaper fades in over 0.3 s, then the OUTLAW / SURVIVORS wordmark punches in (no bounce), while the title loads on a thread; once the hit has settled (about 1.02 s) the title is built under the screen and the screen dissolves (0.25 s) into it, never before 1.15 s. Nothing pauses; no input. The same screen also plays on the way into every run (see Revolver wipe).

What it shows

  • Six vertical strips of dark saloon wallpaper in two tones with pinstripes and beads, printed tone-on-tone with silhouettes of revolvers, sheriff stars, bombs, boots and other western and game items
  • The strips scroll in turn up and down at 46 px/s while the whole set drifts right at 15 px/s
  • The wordmark plate: OUTLAW / SURVIVORS, resting with its middle at 46% of the screen's height
  • The punch: the plate arriving from 2.6 times its size, zoom-blurred, with speed lines closing in on it; a white impact frame; the screen shaking; a shockwave ring bending the wallpaper; dust and chips flying off its edges; a glint across the letters

Rules

  • The plate slams in at 0.3 s and hits its rest at 0.42 s (0.12 s, faster and faster, never past its size and never back: no squash, no bounce). The hit: the flash held 0.05 s (the hit-stop), then fading; a 22 px shake dying out over 0.3 s; from the end of the hit-stop the ring runs 0.42 s, the dust 0.5 s and the chips 0.55 s; the ui_stamp thud over a low boom; the glint 0.18 s after the hit for 0.38 s. Everything is over at 1.02 s; the title is built only after that, and the dissolve never starts before 1.15 s.
  • If the threaded load stalls for 10 s the title is loaded directly; if it cannot load at all a rock-red line says 'The title screen did not load. Please close the game and start it again.', the wallpaper stops moving and the screen stays.
  • Everything is one shader over one full-screen rect, so the dissolve fades every pixel evenly.
  • The camera-cutout ink band waits while this shows and fades in with the title.

States

the wallpaper fading in · the title punching in · the hit (impact frame, shake, ring, chips) · settled, loading · failed load (error line, wallpaper still)

02Play data question

game/ui/wanted/WConsent.gd

Ask once whether anonymous play data may be sent.

When it appears

Over the title, 0.8 s after it comes in, the first time a build that can send analytics starts and the question has not been answered. Closes on an answer; the back gesture leaves it unanswered and it comes again next launch.

What you can do

NO THANKSDeclines: nothing is sent and anything waiting is deleted. The answer is kept in the save, through a wipe.
OKAccepts: what waited is sent. From then on Settings' 'Anonymous play data' switch is the answer.
Dim around the cardDoes nothing: the card wants an answer.

What it shows

  • Star line 'A quick word', title PLAY DATA
  • Body: what is sent (the items you pick, where you fall, how smoothly it runs), that you appear only as your generated player name (e.g. 'Lanky Tinker 24'), that it asks a few questions after a run now and then, and that both can be turned off in Settings

Rules

  • Until answered nothing is sent or written to disk.
  • Not shown at all when the build's analytics key file is empty.

States

first launch, unanswered

03Revolver wipe

game/ui/RunCurtain.gd

Carry the player from a menu into a run without a blank frame: the run loads behind the loading screen.

When it appears

Every way into a run: START, a Stages card, PLAY AGAIN, the arena. The screen left is seen through a revolver's cylinder, end on, that closes on the middle of the screen while it turns until it is dark (0.5 s, the cylinder's spin sound). The loading screen comes out of the dark through the same opening (0.3 s) as the wordmark punches in; the run loads meanwhile and is built under it once the hit has settled. From 1.15 s, once the run has drawn, the cylinder opens from the hero's spawn beam onto the run (0.4 s).

What you can do

Any touchNothing: no tap goes through until it opens.

What it shows

  • The cylinder's five chambers cut out of the dark, turning
  • The loading screen in the middle of the sequence
  • The cylinder opening on the spawn beam

Rules

  • If the run's scene cannot load, a line says 'The run did not load. Back to the title.' and the player goes back.

States

closing on the menu · loading screen · opening on the run

game/ui/StagesScreen.gd

Choose which stage of the map to play; each stage is its own fresh run with the chosen hero.

When it appears

START on the title once the map's first stage has been beaten, on every START from then on. The back square, Esc or the back gesture return to the title.

What you can do

Back squareBack to the title.
START on a stage card (open)Starts that stage as a fresh level-1 run from its window's start.
Stage card (locked)Refused: the locked sound and the card shakes; nothing starts.

What it shows

  • 'Red Canyon · <hero>', the best run ('best 10:00 · Lv 47 · stage 2') and the call 'Choose a Stage'

Stakes (id spite) also gives a hidden +5% XP and coins a rank.
  • The board does not scroll under an open sheet.

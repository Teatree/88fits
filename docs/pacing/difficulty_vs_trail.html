Difficulty against the Trail

Pass 133, lane PACING, 2026-11-07. How hard each stage is for a player at a given Trail Level, where the Trail opens each stage against where a middling player clears it, and the Dust Bowl and Blood Moon written again in the style of Sundown and Nightfall. Every number here comes from a tool run on the game's own files (the HeroPower probe, the difficulty probe, the run sim, the Level Editor's estimate); nothing in the Trail or the stage multipliers was changed.

1. The player's journey as the Trail model sees it

The Trail is 100 nodes on 70 levels (content/trail/trail.json, Garry's file). What the curve says. The Trail is a gentle slope with three flat stretches and one cliff:

  • Levels 1 to 20 give x1.39 at level 1 (x1.31 at outlaw level 30): about 2% a level. Most of it is health (+10%, +15 shield, +4% plate) and damage (+12%).
  • Levels 40 to 45 give no power at all (The Ghost card, Big Game, a banish, grip, May): the same x1.81 at 40 and 45. Levels 20 to 30 are nearly as flat (x1.39 to x1.52: an outlaw, Tumbleweed, Death, a reroll, a healing node).
  • Levels 60 to 65 are the cliff: x2.34 to x3.67 at level 1, almost all of it the +1 projectile at 64 (Extra Barrel doubles a lone Revolver) and the three plain nodes of level 63.
  • Level 70 (node 100) is x4.2 a fresh Drifter at level 1 and x2.97 at level 30.

The purse. A node costs 10 at level 1, 460 at level 8, 2,520 at level 20, 7,840 at level 36, 14,840 at level 50 and 28,570 at level 70; the required nodes to Trail Level 20 cost 20,140 coins in all, to 36 103,710, to 50 261,250, to 70 897,810. What a run banks, measured: the run sim's explorer bot (6 players, the Drifter, a death ends the run) banked 9 coins a Sundown run at Trail 0 and 208 at Trail 15 (it dies at 2:12 in Garry's red goblin horde), 6 to 41 a Nightfall run (it dies at 1:00 to the ten gunmen), 3,470 a Dust Bowl run at Trail 20 and 3,500 at 35, 5,345 a Blood Moon run at 36 and 8,100 at 51 (both without killing the Brander). Real players (42 Sundown runs and 26 Nightfall runs in analytics/remote, mostly from before the Trail): Sundown banks 1,006 on average (median 555; 13 of 42 killed the King), Nightfall 737 (median 18: 13 of 26 fell before the Gila, 3 killed him). So the model's "Sundown 1,200 cleared from level 8, Nightfall 4,000 from 18" (BALANCING 71.TRAILSET) is about right for Sundown and three to five times too kind for Nightfall: a player who can only clear Sundown earns about 1,000 a run and needs 20 runs for Trail Level 20 (the Dust Bowl's node) and 100 runs for level 36 (the Blood Moon's), unless Nightfall starts paying.

Three stretches a stage: the first minute, the middle, the last minutes before the boss. The outlaw's level per stretch is a plain run's (6 / 10-18 / 16-32). The probe is noisy by about 0.15 a cell; read the shape, not the second decimal. Trail Level 0 is a player who bought nothing.

3. The curve: where each stage opens, where it is cleared, the walls and the holes

Where each stage opens and where it is cleared (the numbers above, in one list):

  • Sundown (open from the start): the probe's b1 build at Trail 0 loses 1.5 max HP a minute in the last 100 s (Cyclopean Mummies x12 HP and werewolves) and nothing before; at Trail 10 it is 0.6, at Trail 20 nothing. Real players: 13 of 42 runs killed the King. Clearable from the start, comfortable about Trail 10-15.
  • Nightfall (open once the King falls, no Trail node): at Trail 0 every stretch costs about 1 max HP a minute (the ten gunmen at 0:01 and the spiders, the goblin horde with the Monks at 4:50, the Spit Cactus at 7:50 against a b2 build); at Trail 10 the two later stretches still cost 0.7 and 0.1; at Trail 20 nothing. Clearable about Trail 10, comfortable at 20 for a player with a build; the bot never gets past its first minute. Real players: 3 of 26 runs.
  • The Dust Bowl (the Trail opens it at level 20): with pass 126's rows the probe loses nothing at any Trail Level in any stretch (0.00 at 0, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, Drifter and Calavera): 16-48 creatures alive against Nightfall's 150, the per-body numbers lower too. The bot: 3 of 6 killed Black Sunday at Trail 20 and 3 of 6 at 35 (the other three fell to a special or an Elite mid-stage). It opens at 20 and is already comfortable there for anyone who cleared Nightfall; it is the easiest stage in the game after Sundown's first half. That is the hole this pass fills in Part 2.
  • Blood Moon (the Trail opens it at level 36): the first 13 minutes cost the probe nothing at Trail 0 either; the last stretch (13:10, 125 alive, werewolves, hounds, the knight wall and Spit Cactus) costs 1.3 at Trail 0, 0.0 at 10 and up. The bot at Trail 36: 0 of 6 killed the Brander, median end 13:50 (three fell in the last stretch); at 51: 0 of 6, all six reached him and fell in his fight (median end 16:58). Clearable to the boss from about Trail 20 by the probe's reading and about 40 by the bot's; the Brander himself is the wall (the bot has never killed him at any board, pass 126 said the same), not the rows.

Walls and holes, plainly:

  • A hole at 20: the Dust Bowl opens 10-15 levels after it could be cleared and is easier than the stage before it. The ramp Sundown -> Nightfall -> Dust Bowl -> Blood Moon was Nightfall > Dust Bowl < Blood Moon's last stretch.
  • A hole from 20 to 36: for a player who can clear Nightfall, no stage gets harder for 16 levels (about 84,000 coins, 20 to 80 runs). Sixteen levels in which nothing new resists him.
  • A flat stretch at 40-45 (no power for five levels, 33,000 coins) right after the Blood Moon opens, where he most needs it.
  • A wall at the Brander, and the Trail cannot climb it: the bot reaches him at 36 and at 51 and dies to him both times. His boss_hp_scaling.seconds 80 (the Gila 60, Black Sunday 70) and his ring of fire are what to look at, not the rows.
  • A money wall before 36 for a player who cannot clear Nightfall: 100 Sundown runs to the Blood Moon's node.

4. Garry's style, read off Sundown and Nightfall

Read off the estimate (tools/level_editor/model.js spawnEstimate, the default kill rate) and the rows themselves (tools/dl/p133/PACING/status.md has the per-chapter numbers). What Garry does:

  • One fodder silhouette at a time, in hundreds. A chapter of 60-100 s is one creature in two rows: a waves row of 20-75 in packs (scorpions 20 every 5 s, red goblins 30 every 5 s + 20 every 4 s, cactus walkers 70 a ring every 10 s, werewolves 75 a ring then packs of 15; on Nightfall spiders in packs of 15 every 5 s, goblins 25 every 5 s + packs of 25 every 7 s, scorpions in packs of 45) and a scatter or pack stream. The next silhouette starts as the last one ends, with a short overlap. Hordes are weak per body: hp 20 absolute, x0.2 to x1.3 of the curve.
  • Three or four things with attacks alive, each under a Max alive of 2-6: Spit Cactus (max 5-8), gunmen (max 3), beetles (max 3-6), giants (max 1-2), one giant Bull Beetle in front at 2:40, one Shielded giant at 4:52, Monk squads of 20-35 in front (one on Sundown, three on Nightfall), plus the map's Elites (every 129 s) and specials (every 64-120 s, one alive).
  • A ring every two and a half to three and a half minutes, fodder only, 30-75 strong: Sundown 0:47, 4:05 (18 m), 5:17; Nightfall 2:58, 7:11 (15 m) and small beetle / mummy rings. In-front walls as often again.
  • A quiet minute between chapters where the crowd falls to a third: Sundown 0:53-2:00, 3:30-5:00, 6:30-7:10; Nightfall 0:00-2:00, 4:00-5:00, 8:00-10:00.
  • Peaks of 235-260 alive, a mean of about 100, 3-6 rows open at once (Nightfall up to 9).
  • A thinner, tankier last stretch: Cyclopean Mummies x12 HP in sevens for Sundown's last minute; big slow scorpions, giants and Spit Cactus for Nightfall's last two.
  • The boss fight window: two fodder trickles (packs of 2-4 every 2.5-4 s), a ranged pair every 40-60 s (max 2-3), a beetle charge at 0:45-0:50, a ring of 12 at 1:15; max 25-30 bodies.

Pass 126's Dust Bowl and Blood Moon did the opposite: three fodder species mixed all the time in small waves (6-9 every 5-6 s), 5-11 rows open at once, ranged rows the whole stage, no quiet minute (the Dust Bowl sits at 38-60 alive for five minutes), peaks of 152 / 205, and more rings than his (5 + a wall in 13 min; 8 in 16 min).

7. Proposals for Garry (nothing of this is changed)

Garry's to decide; none of it is in this pass:

  1. Open the Dust Bowl at Trail Level 12-15, not 20 (move node n024 down), now that its rows resist: the probe and the bot both say a player who clears Nightfall is ready for it, and Nightfall is clearable from about Trail 10. Alternatively keep 20 and make Nightfall the stage that pays: today it pays a median of 18 coins because 13 of 26 real runs fall before the Gila.
  2. Give levels 41-45 one power node (today The Ghost, Big Game, a banish, grip and May: five levels and 33,000 coins with no power right after the Blood Moon opens). Swapping level 42's Big Game with level 51's Tempo +5% would do it; the "no type twice within five levels" rule holds.
  3. The Brander, not the rows, is the Blood Moon's wall: the bot reaches him at Trail 36 and 51 and never kills him. Options in his data: boss_hp_scaling.seconds 80 -> 60-70 (the Gila's and Black Sunday's), or his ring of fire's burn. Measure with _p110_BOSS_fight_probe.gd before and after.
  4. The price curve's k (7.2) is fitted to a purse model that gives Nightfall 4,000 a run; real runs bank 737 on average there. If Nightfall stays as it is, k 5.0-5.5 keeps "about 100 runs to node 100" true for a player whose income is Sundown's 1,000 plus the Dust Bowl's 3,500 from Trail 20.
  5. The stage multipliers [1, 2.2, 2.05, 2.05] can stay: the Dust Bowl's and the Blood Moon's thinness was the rows, and the rows now carry it.

8. What is doubtful

  • The probe plays a fixed strong build with a kiting autopilot and never dies; its zeroes mean "no pressure on a built hero", not "a fresh hero strolls through". The bot is the other extreme (it cannot pass Sundown's goblin horde). A human sits between; the Dust Bowl's "0 lost at every Trail Level" and the bot's 3 of 6 clears together are the evidence it was too thin.
  • One seed a cell (seed 11), 90 s a cell: the probe's own noise is about 0.15 max HP a minute; cells under 0.2 are "nothing".
  • The estimate's alive counts use the Level Editor's default kill rate (0.06 a second); in play a built hero kills faster and the hordes read lower. The shape (one silhouette, quiet minutes, rings) is what it shows well.
  • The damage_mult raise (Dust Bowl fodder to 1.0, the Blood Moon's to 0.8-0.85) is my reading of "in Garry's style" (his rows run 1.0-3.0); the after-probe is the check. If a phone run feels harsh in the Dust Bowl's crow chapters (8:00-10:30), those two rows' damage_mult is the knob.
  • Black Sunday's and the Brander's fights were not re-measured (the windows are unchanged but for one trickle).
  • No real player has played the Dust Bowl or the Blood Moon with the Trail yet (one quit Dust Bowl run in the analytics).

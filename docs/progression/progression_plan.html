The Fifty-Run Plan

What a new player has open on their first run, what they unlock by choosing a weapon, a Card or an outlaw and taking it far, and a Trail they finish in about fifty runs, a little stronger at the end than today. Version 2 follows Garry's notes on version 1. Coins x25 is off for every number on this page.

What changed in this version

  1. 1. No structures or Bounty Board on the Trail. Every structure stays open and the Bounty Board opens after one finished run, as today. Their six round-1 slots are power nodes now (Dead Eye +2% at 3, Aegis +10 at 13, Aegis +10 at 27, Plate +2% at 42, Omen +4% at 44, Plate +2% at 56). Read as: "features" = the Bounty Board. Berserk and the Nuke are power-ups, neither: they stay on the Trail (Q2).
  2. 2. Every legendary locked at the start, more of them early. All 23 rare, epic and legendary items are locked; the six legendaries sit at Trail Levels 10, 19, 31, 43, 55 and 66 (the first by about run 6, the second by run 12), the three Trail epics at 12, 17 and 26, five more epics and seven rares behind tasks from run 3 on. The start has every common and nine uncommons, so a first run still has an item for every stat family (section 2 says why it does not feel like missing out).
  3. 3. One first-run task. Dust Yourself Off (finish a run) unlocks the Tommy Gun. First Blood, Growing Up, Card Sharp and Lucky Find are gone. What they gave comes at run 2-3 at the earliest: The Tower and Pocket Watch at Trail Levels 4-5 (run 2), The Star and Powder Keg at 6-7 (run 3). Every other task's need went up or became a choice (Killing Spree 1,000 to 2,000 kills, Old Hand 3 to 10 runs).
  4. 4. Tasks are choices. 34 tasks; 27 ask for a chosen weapon, Card, item or outlaw taken far (level 5, level 4, a rung, an evolution, a total while using it). The generic counters are one ladder that shows one step at a time. 12 tasks sit behind another task (Big Game: 5 bosses holding Big Game Hunter, after Gila Hunter unlocks it). Split of the locked things as asked: items 15 / 15, weapons 6 / 4, Cards 8 / 4, outlaws 4 / 1, skins 1 / 10 (five of the ten skin tasks behind another task). A normal run ends with 1-3 task rows on the Results; 5-6 only for a player chasing several (a display rule does the rest). Read as: "It should be mindless" = it should NOT be mindless. The ratios are Trail / tasks among the locked things of each kind, after point 8.
  5. 5. Runs 1 to 50. Run 1 now falls at about 3:00 with one unlock (the Tommy Gun); the first Trail unlocks come on run 2. The runs table colours every unlock by kind with a letter, and marks a Trail node ◆ and a task ✓. Read as: likely, not certain. What makes 3:00 likely: Sundown's bite x0.95 to x1.0 over the first three minutes (today x1.21), a 33-thing start pool, the same learning curve.
  6. 6. The Trail's mix kept. 12 choice groups (28 nodes), 10 optional nodes, 3 nodes where rails meet: all as today. Plain nodes 62 to 92 (the new unlocks and power), 38 unlock nodes (today 16).
  7. 7. Each stage clearly the better farm. Coins a real minute for a clear: Sundown 151, Nightfall 567, the Dust Bowl 1077, Blood Moon 2085 (today 126, 356, 460, 690). A fall half-way through Nightfall (233 a minute) already beats a Sundown clear. Posters harder and richer by stage (reward x1, x3, x7, x14; counts x1, x1.6, x2.2, x3). Prices refitted to these purses.
  8. 8. 20% fewer locks, every kind alike. 84 locked things in round 1, 68 now: weapons 12 to 10, Cards 15 to 12, items 37 to 30, outlaws 6 to 5, skins 14 to 11. A first run's draw is 33 things (round 1: 20, today: 63).

Warnings

  • The first run's 3:00 rests on the difficulty change. Without Sundown's softer first three minutes the model's run 1 falls at about 2:10 (today's first runs: median 2:26, five deaths at 0:20-0:40). The start pool alone will not carry it.
  • Prices only fit these purses. The curve is steeper again (power 1.90) because later stages pay far more. On round 1's purses this Trail takes 93 runs, on today's 110. The stage pay (coin multipliers, boss purses, Wages, posters) and the prices must land together.
  • Chained tasks need new code. A task behind another (requires), "holding an item" (holding), "as an outlaw" on a level or rung task (hero) and a boss count (bosses_total) do not exist today; QuestDef and QuestTracker have no prerequisite. Twelve tasks wait on it.
  • Chained tasks a player never sees. A task behind another is hidden until that one is done; a player who never takes Big Game Hunter never meets Big Game. The Collection should show a locked task's name and what it waits for, or players will think the item cannot be had.
  • Task clutter needs a display rule too. The Results lists every task whose progress grew (ResultsScreen.progress_list, no cap). With 27 choice tasks a run holding five weapons and five Cards moves 4-8 of them. Proposed: at most 3 rows (the ladder's one, then the two the run moved most).
  • Fewer locks, but still more than today for items (30 against 5) and Cards (12 against 7): runs 1-5 offer 33 to 45 things against today's 63. If runs 2-3 feel samey, open 3-4 more uncommons.
  • Power at the end is 110% of today's (eight new power nodes: the six slots, Astro's and May's). Take two of them out to land on 100%.
  • Old saves lose things they had if the new pool applies to them; a once-migration should keep them (Q3).
  • Skins cost gold (1,500-3,000 each, about 33,000 for all) on top of the Trail's 715,270; the model does not count them.
  • The model is thin late (no real Blood Moon run): fifty could be forty or seventy; k is the one number to refit.

Decide these first

  1. The stage pay (section 5): coins x1.33 / x2 / x2.85 on the later stages, boss purses x1.5 / x2 / x2, Wages 30 / 80 / 150 / 240 a minute, posters x1 / x3 / x7 / x14. The prices only fit with it.
  2. The new task code: requires, holding, hero, bosses_total and the Results rule (section 3).
  3. Sundown's first three minutes softer (section 6), so run 1 lasts to about 3:00.
  4. Old saves keep what they had open (Q3).

Why a first run does not feel short of anything. Legendary items are never offered before 4:30 of a stage (pass 121) and a first run falls at about 3:00, so locking them costs a new player nothing they would have seen. Epics and legendaries weigh 5-6 in a draw against 8-10 for commons and uncommons: even open, a first run meets one rarely. The start keeps one item for every stat family (health, damage, size, speed, healing, fire, poison, freeze, coins). And the first rare comes at run 3, the first epic and legendary around runs 6-7.

How a run ends with 1-3 task rows, not 6

  • One generic counter at a time: the ladder (finish a run, 2,000 kills, 10 runs, 25,000 coins, 25,000 kills). Today 9 generic totals grow every run (kills, runs, chests, minutes, barrels, Totems...).
  • Everything else needs a choice: a task moves only with the weapon, Card, item or outlaw it names. Skin tasks move only for their outlaw's player.
  • A display rule (new, ResultsScreen.progress_list): at most 3 progress rows, the ladder's first, then the two tasks the run moved most towards done. A player chasing one unlock sees it every run; a player chasing a build (May's Tommy Gun, Orbit Saws, The Giant) can see 5-6 moved and still gets the 3 nearest.
  • Run 1 ends with Dust Yourself Off done (and Hold the Line for a player who never lifts the thumb for 2 minutes).

7Risks and exploits

  • Farming Sundown: answered by point 7. A Sundown clear pays 151 a real minute, a half Nightfall 233, a Nightfall clear 567.
  • Farming the Dust Bowl or Blood Moon in Endless: no Wages after the boss and rare coins; the stage's pay is in its clock and its boss, not in Endless.
  • Posters: one a run; at x14 a Blood Moon poster pays up to 4,340, about 12% of a clear. If posters become the reason to play, lower the top multiplier first.
  • The Stakes: eight optional nodes, +5% hidden coins and XP each; a strong player takes some runs off the fifty. Intended.
  • Task farming: a total task (fire, poison, healing) could be farmed in Endless; each is a one-time unlock, so it costs nothing.

The Fifty-Run Plan

What a new player has open on their first run, what they unlock in their first two minutes and their first five runs, and a Trail they finish in about fifty runs instead of a hundred, with the same power at the end. Every number says where it comes from. Coins x25 is off for every number on this page.

Decide these first

  1. Wages. Every run pays coins a minute of the stage clock (30 / 60 / 90 / 120 by stage), on top of the purse. Without it the plan takes 60 runs, not 50, and a 2-minute death still banks about 40 coins. Coins x25 goes when this lands. (Q1)
  2. A first run with 6 weapons, 6 Cards and 8 items, the rest behind 20 quests and 44 new Trail nodes. (section 2)
  3. The price curve base 20, k 9.17, power 1.55 (today 40, 7.2, 1.95): a level costs about one run's purse all the way, so every run buys something. (section 5)
  4. Old saves keep what they had open, or everyone starts on the new pool. (Q3)
  5. Harder stages from the Dust Bowl on (creature health and attack +20% and +30% at the start, steeper inside every stage, a softer first 40 seconds on Sundown), then measured. (section 6)

Runs 1 to 50

The journey model (plan.py): before each run the player has the power their Trail Level gives (HeroPower, level-30 build); they play the open stage that pays best, and tries the next one as soon as they have a quarter chance at its boss. That chance comes from the stage's demand band (section 6) times their own learning (15% on run 1, 77% by run 5, 90% by run 8). The purse is the expected one: clear chance x a cleared purse + the rest x a fallen purse, Wages and a poster included. After the run they buy Trail nodes in order while the coins last. 5The Trail in fifty runs

Why it takes so long today. Two things. The price of a level grows as level1.95: level 70 costs 62 times level 8, while the purse grows about 11 times from a Sundown clear (1,100) to a Blood Moon clear (12,000). So a level costs a third of a run early and three runs late. And the purse model it was fitted to is far kinder than real runs early: a Sundown run that falls before the King banks a median of 13 coins (mean 72, 36 runs; runs.py), the model said 34 for run 1 and 1,223 for run 5. Version 1.00 newcomers banked 0 to 186 a run in their first six runs; that is why Coins x25 exists.

What changes

  1. Wages (new rule): a run's end pays coins for every minute of the stage clock survived: Sundown 30, Nightfall 60, the Dust Bowl 90, Blood Moon 120 a minute. A 2-minute fall on Sundown banks about 75 instead of 15; a King kill about 1,300 instead of 1,100; a Blood Moon clear about 14,000. The clock stops at the boss, so Endless pays no Wages. It moves the early runs most: without it the plan takes 60 runs.
  2. The price curve base 20, k 9.17, power 1.55, round 10 (today 40, 7.2, 1.95), fitted by plan.py so the last level falls on run 50 with levels 1-8 done by run 4-5. A plain node costs 250 at level 8 (today 460), 970 at 20 (2,520), 2,390 at 36 (7,840), 3,960 at 50 (14,840), 6,660 at 70 (28,570). Every required node: 291,445 coins (today 897,810).
  3. Outlaws priced by their level (twice a plain node's price: Astro 600 at level 9, Calavera 1,380 at 16, May 6,740 at 45) instead of the fixed 1,500 to 4,000, so the early ones do not stall a new player for three runs (Q4).
  4. The stages two levels down: the Dust Bowl at 18 (was 20), Blood Moon at 34 (was 36); Aegis +15 and Turret Buddy take their places. The bosses before them still hold the door.
  5. Power moved, not removed: Extra Barrel (+1 projectile) from level 64 to 47 (Bounty +15% goes to 64), level 63's Tempo +6% to 42 and Wide Load +10% to 44, Kill Shot +20% from 58 to 40. This fills the flat stretch after the Blood Moon's gate (pass 133 found 40-45 gave nothing) and takes the cliff out of the last ten levels.
  6. 44 unlock nodes at half price (section 2).

The node amounts stay Garry's: the end power is exactly today's node 100 (x2.90 a fresh save at level 30, x4.12 at level 1). Nothing is taken away, so nothing at the top is weaker; the fifty runs come from cheaper levels and fuller early purses.

7Risks and exploits

  • Farming Sundown. A King kill pays about 1,300 in 7 minutes (185 a minute); a Nightfall clear 4,700 in 10 (470), the Dust Bowl about 7,800 in 13 (600), Blood Moon about 14,000 in 16 (870). Pay a minute rises with every stage, so going back is slower. A deliberate 2-minute death loop earns about 40 a real minute with the menus: no exploit.
  • Endless. No Wages (the clock stands still) and its coins are rare (coin_chance 0.05), so a long Endless is XP, not coins. Watch the boss chest's speed bonus (pass 118), not Endless.
  • The posters. One a run, 100-300 coins; the model counts half of them done. With Coins x25 gone they stay a side income, about 5% of a late purse.
  • The Stakes. Eight optional nodes at a quarter price, each +5% hidden coins and XP for +10% creature strength: a strong player can take ten or so runs off the fifty. That is their point; the plan does not count them.
  • Coin items early (Pickpocket at the start, Hush Money at Trail Level 5, Loose Change at level 10) shorten the plan a little for a player who takes them. Fine, and a reason to take them.
  • A small pool feels samey on runs 2-3 if the quests do not fire: the first six all land in run 1 or 2 on the measured numbers.

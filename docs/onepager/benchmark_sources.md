### The market-gap finding

Megabonk is PC-only, and no phone game we found puts its third-person chase camera (one that turns with the hero) in portrait. 3D survivor-likes in portrait do exist and some are big (Archero 1 and 2, Monster Survivors, Zombie Waves, Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor since Nov 2025), but every one frames the fight from a fixed high top-down or three-quarter camera; the nearest in feel is Voodoo's Monster Survivors (3D cliffs and ziplines, 10M+ installs). The only survivor-likes with a third-person chase camera we found on phones are landscape, with 1K-10K installs.

What is true:

- Megabonk (117,336 peak players, 1M copies in 2 weeks) is on Windows and Linux only; neither store lists it (searched 'megabonk' on the US App Store and Google Play on 2026-10-26) [M1, K15, K16, K17].
- The 3D + portrait corner is occupied, and by big games: Archero (50M+), Monster Survivors (10M+), Zombie Waves (10M+), Archero 2 (5M+), DRG: Survivor mobile, Mech Assemble and Z Survivor (1M+ each).
- None of them has Megabonk's camera, a third-person chase camera that turns with the hero: they are fixed and north-up (Zombie Waves sits behind the hero but never turns). On Steam, Megabonk is also by far the biggest 'Bullet Heaven' + 'Third Person' game (58,700 English reviews; the next, Jotunnslayer, 3,056) [K14].

What the deck must not say:

- '88fits is the only 3D survivor-like in portrait': false (see the overlap list).
- 'There are no 3D survivor-likes on mobile': false.

Claims that hold up:

- 'Megabonk is not on phones. 88fits brings its kind of run (stages that end in a boss and a portal, a 3D world with shrines and quests) to portrait, for one thumb.'
- 'The biggest portrait survivor-likes are 2D (Survivor.io, Vampire Survivors) or top-down 3D with rooms (Archero).'
- Do not claim that no phone game has Megabonk's run structure: we did not play Monster Survivors, Zombie Waves or the others, we read their store text and screenshots.

88fits' default camera is itself fixed and high (pitch 46 deg, north up; a behind-the-hero 'Follow' camera is an option). Its difference from Monster Survivors or DRG: Survivor mobile is the Megabonk run structure, the painted anime characters on 3D ground and the western world, not the camera alone.

Outlaw Survivors · design proposal · pass 118

Twelve legendary items

Ideas for the first legendary passive items, borrowed in spirit from Megabonk, Vampire Survivors, Survivor.io and Lonely Survivor, and dressed for the frontier. Each one climbs the ladder once: Legendary when found, Evolved on its one upgrade. Nothing here is built yet; pick the ones you like and they become content folders.

How rare a legendary card is today

The card pool already weighs legendaries at 0.15 of a common (CardPool RARITY_W), and luck lifts them by 1 + 2 × luck. Against the 79 weapons, cards and passive items in the pool now, one legendary item of weight 5 would show up on:

1 in 208level-up offers with no luck
1 in 130with +30% luck (three ranks of Clover)
1 in 95with +60% luck
~1 run in 6sees one at all (about 35 offers a stage, no luck)

That rarity is what lets them be loud. Each bill below says why its numbers hold anyway, so that the one run that finds it feels special without being won on the spot. Suggested weights are 4 to 6, and none offered before minute 2 of a stage.

Dead Man's Hand

Aces and eights. Somebody's not walking out.

Legendary
Every 8th hit of each weapon is a Dead Man's Shot: ×5 damage, and it can still crit. A black card with aces and eights flicks over the target.
Evolved
Every 6th hit, still ×5.

Inspired by

Megabonk's Big Bonk, which rolls a small chance of ×20 damage. A count instead of a dice roll makes it something you can plan around.

The choice and the fun

It pulls you toward weapons that hit often (the Tommy Gun, the Orbit Saws, the Salt) over slow heavy ones, and pairs with crit items. On a boss you can feel the rhythm of the big numbers coming.

Why it won't break balance

It averages +50% damage at Legendary and +67% Evolved, which is in line with a maxed Ghost Pepper (+69% might) and has no build of its own behind it. Bosses already scale their HP to your recent DPS, so it can't delete them.

Why it won't hurt performance

One counter per weapon, read on a hit that is already being dealt. The flick reuses the damage-number layer, which already merges and thins past 40 on screen.

High Noon Bell

When it tolls, the whole town holds its breath.

Legendary
Every 60 s the bell tolls and time stops for 3 s: creatures, their shots and the spawns freeze. The hero walks, picks up and keeps firing.
Evolved
Every 45 s for 4 s, and kills during the stop drop double XP.

Inspired by

Vampire Survivors' Orologion, which freezes every enemy on screen.

The choice and the fun

Each toll is a window you decide how to spend: break out of a ring, sweep a field of gems, or stand in a boss's face and empty every gun. Players will start counting the seconds to the next one.

Why it won't break balance

It stops about 5% of the run (9% Evolved). The stage clock and the spawns stop with the crowd, so it never skips the hard parts of the timeline. Boss HP follows your DPS, so a burn window speeds a fight up a little and doesn't trivialise it.

Why it won't hurt performance

A frozen crowd costs less than a moving one: it reuses the arena's crowd freeze, so nothing steers for those seconds.

The Iron Horse

The 4:10 doesn't stop for anybody.

Legendary
Every 20 s a whistle blows and a track lights up across the screen through you, along the way you face. One second later a ghost train charges down it. Everything on the rails takes 25% of its max HP (Elites and bosses 5%) plus might-scaled damage, and is thrown aside.
Evolved
Every 15 s, and two trains in a cross.

Inspired by

Vampire Survivors' Pentagram screen wipes, made directional so you aim them.

The choice and the fun

The whistle is a promise: in that second you turn to line the crowd up on the rails. Done well it's the most satisfying clear in the game, and it's a spectacle other players will want to see.

Why it won't break balance

It hits one line, not the screen, and it's capped against Elites and bosses. It clears about as much as the Nuke power-up, on a fixed timer.

Why it won't hurt performance

One moving mesh and one line query through the existing creature grid for under a second; the warning is the Telegraphs line the bosses already use.

Pocket Bible

Stopped a slug meant for a better man.

Legendary
No single hit takes more than 12% of your max HP. Once every 20 s a hit is stopped outright, and the HUD icon shows a fresh bullet hole.
Evolved
9%, and a hit stopped every 15 s.

Inspired by

Vampire Survivors' Crimson Shroud, which caps the damage you take.

The choice and the fun

It lets glass cannons (Ghost Pepper, Last Stand) survive the big hits. It also fights Desperado, which wants you low, so it is a real fork in the build.

Why it won't break balance

Crowds hurt you with many small bites, which it barely touches. It removes one-shots (the Gila's slam, the Sniper's beam, the Raider's rocket, a dynamite blast), not the slow bleed that actually ends runs.

Why it won't hurt performance

One comparison in the hero's damage intake.

Two-Headed Coin

Heads I win. Tails, also heads.

Legendary
Every chest's reel lands twice and you take both. The second spin is free and can't be skipped by mistake.
Evolved
Purple chests and the bosses' chests also pay 50% more gold.

Inspired by

Vampire Survivors' chests that open three or five items at once, and Megabonk's chest economy.

The choice and the fun

Chests become worth crossing the map for. It pairs with the new Jade and Ruby Keys: one chest is still one key tick, but twice the loot.

Why it won't break balance

Chests are rare now (regular creatures drop one at 0.02%; specials and Elites are the main source), so it is worth four to eight extra levels a stage. Strong for a legendary, not a win.

Why it won't hurt performance

The chest screen spins its reel a second time. Nothing in the world changes.

Fool's Gold

It ain't gold till you spend it.

Legendary
+1% damage for every 50 gold in this run's purse, up to +60%. Spending at the Shop or with the Stranger lowers it.
Evolved
+1% for every 40 gold, and +1% max HP for every 100.

Inspired by

Vampire Survivors' Stone Mask and the gold-for-power items in Megabonk.

The choice and the fun

A real tension every time a shop opens: buy the upgrade now, or keep the purse heavy for damage. The bosses' new jackpot chests make it swing hard late in a stage.

Why it won't break balance

It's capped. It counts only this run's purse, never banked gold. It starts weak and peaks late, which is when legendary items should peak.

Why it won't hurt performance

It is recomputed when the gold changes (a signal the HUD already listens to), never every frame.

Undertaker's Shovel

Every grave's a job. Every job's a hand.

Legendary
One kill in 25 leaves a grave. Two seconds later a ghost gunslinger climbs out and fights where it stands for 8 s, firing your first weapon's shots at 40% damage. At most 4 at once.
Evolved
One kill in 15, at most 6 ghosts, 12 s each.

Inspired by

The summoner items of Survivor.io and Lonely Survivor.

The choice and the fun

A summoner build in a game that has none. Ghosts stay by their graves, so where you fight decides where your army stands, and holding a choke point becomes a plan.

Why it won't break balance

It's capped at 4 or 6, and each ghost deals 40% of one weapon, so it scales with your build and doesn't replace it.

Why it won't hurt performance

At most 6 extra shooters, built on the Turret Buddy's Spawner and Turret behaviour that already ships.

Lightning in a Bottle

Caught it once. It's been sore about it since.

Legendary
Every critical hit throws an arc to up to 3 creatures within 5 m, for 35% of the hit. Arcs never chain again.
Evolved
5 creatures, 50%, and each arc stuns for 0.3 s.

Inspired by

Megabonk's Lightning Orb and Vampire Survivors' Lightning Ring.

The choice and the fun

It turns crit builds, which are great against single targets, into crowd clearers. Arcs that jump across a packed ring look and sound like a jackpot.

Why it won't break balance

It needs crits to work, it never chains, and each arc is a share of a hit you already landed.

Why it won't hurt performance

Arcs are capped at 12 per tenth of a second. Lookups go through the existing 2.5 m grid, and the visuals reuse the Zap Beam's lines.

Bounty on Your Head

Every gun in the territory wants a piece of you.

Legendary
Creatures arrive 35% faster, up to the map's cap, and hit 10% harder. Every kill pays ×1.6 XP and coins.
Evolved
50% faster, ×2 XP and coins.

Inspired by

Vampire Survivors' Curse and Skull O'Maniac, and Megabonk's difficulty-for-reward items.

The choice and the fun

A pure risk dial. Greedy players grab it early and race the curve; careful ones leave it. It's a different flavour of risk from Hot Sauce, which brings tougher variants rather than more of everything.

Why it won't break balance

The map's cap stays, so it can't flood the screen. The harder hits keep the reward earned, and it pays only for kills you actually make.

Why it won't hurt performance

There are never more bodies than the map's hard cap allows; the cap just stays full more often.

Dust Devil in a Jar

Uncork it at a dead run.

Legendary
While you run, a whirlwind grows behind you and reaches full size after 3 s. It pulls gems and coins in from 6 m and tosses the creatures it touches. When you stand still, it shrinks away.
Evolved
Full size in 2 s, wider, and it hurts what it tosses.

Inspired by

Megabonk's movement items, where speed itself is a weapon.

The choice and the fun

It forks the build between running and standing. Siesta and Steady Hand reward standing still; this rewards never stopping. You can't have both.

Why it won't break balance

Its value is mostly pickup range and crowd control, not damage. It needs constant motion, and a ring of creatures stops that.

Why it won't hurt performance

One tornado on the map's existing Dust Devils look, plus one radius query a tick.

Gunslinger's Shadow

He's always one step behind you. Or ahead.

Legendary
Your shadow peels off and moves as your mirror image across the spot where it rose, firing your first weapon at 50% damage. It can't be hurt.
Evolved
It fires your first two weapons and picks up gems and coins it walks over.

Inspired by

Vampire Survivors' mirrored familiars (Peachone and Ebony Wings) and Lonely Survivor's clone.

The choice and the fun

Two bodies to steer with one thumb: walk so that the crowd ends up between you and the twin, in a crossfire. It's the kind of trick players show their friends.

Why it won't break balance

It's 50% of one weapon (two Evolved), and it can't tank or block anything.

Why it won't hurt performance

One more billboard and one weapon emitter, which is less than a single extra weapon slot.

Treasure Map

X marks the spot. The spot is surrounded.

Legendary
An X appears on the sand 40 to 60 m away, with a pointer at the screen's edge. Stand on it for 3 s to dig up a purple chest and 200 gold. A new X appears 90 s after one is dug.
Evolved
Dug in 1.5 s, and the treasure also holds a power-up.

Inspired by

Megabonk's shrines and chests worth hunting across the map.

The choice and the fun

It pulls you out of your safe loop and makes you stand still under pressure while you dig. It pairs with the Keys and the Two-Headed Coin into a whole "treasure hunter" run.

Why it won't break balance

At most one chest every 90 s or so plus modest gold, and the dig costs you the safety of moving.

Why it won't hurt performance

One ground marker and one edge pointer; the pointer is the kit's existing WPointer.

What building them takes

  • Each item is a content folder like any passive item: rarity: "legendary", so the ladder gives it two levels, Legendary and Evolved, plus an icon and an Evolved icon.
  • Six need a small new primitive (a hit counter, a periodic freeze, a damage cap, a gold-to-stat link, a crit proc, a dig site). Two need a new behaviour (the train, the shadow twin). The rest reuse what ships: dust devils, the Spawner and Turret, the spawn knobs, the chest screen.
  • This pass adds the first legendary to the game, the Grindstone (every weapon upgrade grinds in an extra bonus). How it plays will tell us how loud the rest can be.

Numbers are starting points for the Level Editor, not tuned values.

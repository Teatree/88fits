The flow
Launch→Title · swipe hero→PLAY→Run · HUD⇄Level-up / Path / Chest / Shop / Stranger→Stage clear→Results→PLAY AGAIN
MERGED
Play setup → Title (swipe). Tap to continue → the gate at the end of every pick. Pause · Options → Settings, keeping only sound, music, shake, abandon. Hidden collection, debug buttons, hit log, pause log, save path → one developer menu.
REMOVED
Debug · Arena on the title. The version label on the title. Kills · alive and the stage name from the HUD. Empty and locked slots. The kind tag, level chip and flavour line from in-run cards. "Copy the log". Three red "not enough" lines.
REORDERED
Results leads with the hero and the gold, tables last. Stage clear leads with the two roads, not two sentences. The Mirror opens on the board, not the first card. The Collection opens on a grid with counts.
FIRST RUN
Launch → title with the Drifter chosen → PLAY (one tap, 4 s in) → HUD with three hints in the toast slot over the first 30 s: "drag anywhere to walk", "he shoots by himself", "the purple is XP". First level-up at ~0:25 with a fourth hint on the card frame: "the seal is how rare it is". No tutorial screen.
Notes for the build
CANVAS 1080 × 1920
Side margin 48. Modal header at y 110, content from y 470, buttons in the bottom 90–210. Menu back 96 × 96 at (36, 44); title 72 px slab. Thumb zone: nothing interactive between y 700 and 1600 in a run.
COLOUR
paper #F3E6C8 · aged #E2CFA4 · ink #241B14 · brass #C9932F · brass dark #A8792A · rock #B4472B · sage #6E8B5E · turquoise #3F8F8A · ember #D9662A · plum #4A3358 · rare #3E6E9E · epic #8A4FB0 · common #8A8F7A · XP #8A4FB0 · shield #3E6E9E · health #6E8B5E. Sepia dim rgba(48,30,14,.78); paper wash rgba(243,230,200,.82).
TYPE
Alfa Slab One (OFL): titles 150, screen names 72, card names 60, buttons 34–84, numbers 44–110. Bitter (OFL): effect text 30, notes 26, labels 20 tracked +15 %. Minimum 26 px on the phone.
SHAPES
No corner radius on panels; 10–14 px on icon plates; circles for seals, portraits, pause and the coin. Borders 4 px (small), 5 px (cards), 6 px (hero panels). Hard ink shadow offset 5/8/10/12 px by size, no blur. Pressed = shadow to 0, translate by the shadow. Locked = aged paper, 45 %.
BUILD ONCE
Icon plate (size, kind colour, level tag, path rivets). Wax seal (rarity). Item card (plate + seal + kind word + name + stars + effect; optional price button). Slab button (primary / secondary / danger / pressed / locked). Tab strip. Slider with value thumb. Switch. Two-way choice. Modal header (star line + slab title + italic line). Menu header. Toast, banner, edge pointer. Nine-slice: paper panel with 5 px ink border and 8 px shadow; brass button; wax seal.
ART TO PAINT
Full-body hero cut-outs (520 × 700), the Stranger (840 × 760), strongbox and portal cards (180 × 180), a chest for the loot screen, the mirror frame. Icons stay; their dark outlines sit well on brass, sage, rock, plum and turquoise plates; on paper they need the plate.
